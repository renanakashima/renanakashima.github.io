I'm a first-year M.S. student in Computer Science at [Georgia Institute of Technology](https://www.gatech.edu/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"}. Currently, I work on household robotics in Dr. Sonia Chernova's [RAIL Lab](https://www.gt-rail.com/). I'm also grateful to have been awarded the [ITO Foundation U.S.A.–FUTI Scholarship](https://www.friendsofutokyo.org/en/scholarships-awards/scholarships-for-mid-to-long-term-studies/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"} for 2026, which provides full-tuition support for my master's studies at Georgia Tech.

My research interests lie in robot intelligence, particularly in enabling robots to perceive, reason, and act in open-world environments with the flexibility and generality that come naturally to humans.

Previously, I was a full-time research intern at [OMRON SINIC X Corporation](https://www.omron.com/sinicx/en/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"}, where I developed and evaluated approaches to zero-shot robotic manipulation, integrating vision-language models with perception, task-and-motion planning, and motion control in simulated environments.

I received my B.Eng. from the [University of Tokyo](https://www.u-tokyo.ac.jp/en/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"} and spent a year studying Computer Science at [EPFL](https://www.epfl.ch/en/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"} as an exchange student.

Outside the lab, you'll likely find me at an opera or classical music concert.
